# Photorealistic AI media require disclosure

## Statement
Meta's policy requires that photorealistic AI-generated media — video and audio
— be disclosed when published. The obligation is on the media, not on the text
beside it, and it is an obligation to declare rather than a prohibition on
publishing.

## Applies when
always

## Influences
- S-14 — whether a published item declares the AI media it carries.

## Conflicts
None known. This is tier 1; no client rule reaches past it.

## Source
`docs/editorial/CANONICAL_EDITORIAL_MAP_v1.md` §8, row `K-DST-META-02`
(platform documentation, platform rule, tier 1).
