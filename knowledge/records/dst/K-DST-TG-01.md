# Telegram has no feed, so the first line is the whole offer

## Statement
A Telegram channel has no recommendation feed: the reader has already
subscribed, sees the post in order, and leaves when the channel wastes their
time. Growth comes through forwards rather than through ranking, and a long
post is increasingly met by an AI summary rather than by the post itself. All
three point the same way — the thesis belongs in the first line and the first
paragraph, in the words the channel would want quoted.

## Applies when
destination is telegram
