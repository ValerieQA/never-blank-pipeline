# The article is read by scanning before it is read by reading

## Statement
A reader arriving at the canonical article scans it before deciding to read it.
Subheadings that state a conclusion rather than name a topic, the main point
before the background, and sources visible where the claim is made all survive
that scan; a subheading that names a topic tells a scanning reader nothing.

## Applies when
destination is wix

## Influences
- S-10 · `E-14.subheadings` — a subheading states the conclusion of its section.
- S-10 · `E-14.segments` — the main point comes before the background.
- S-10 · `E-14.citations` — the source sits with the claim it supports.
