# Duplicates and an unoriginal stream lose recommendation eligibility

## Statement
Meta's originality policy demotes duplicated posts and posts that are another
account's material with minor edits, and takes recommendation eligibility from
an account whose stream is largely unoriginal. Instagram assesses the stream
over a month rather than post by post, so a single original post does not undo
a month of reposts.

## Applies when
destination is instagram

## Influences
- S-10 · `E-14.exemplars` — a reference is a model for form, never material to
  republish.
- S-13 — near-exact republication of an earlier text.
