# Generalization from a single case

## Statement
The tempting inadmissible interpretation takes what one documented case shows
and states it of a class: of businesses, of owners, of an industry. One case is
evidence of a mechanism and reaches level 2 of the strength ladder; the
generalized reading asserts at level 4 on the same evidence, which is exactly
what the ladder caps.

## Probe
Is there a reading that states of a class — businesses, owners, an industry —
what the evidence documents in one case?

## Real examples

  everywhere: after a major platform changed how it displayed content,

put, and this family is how the probe finds the reading that ignores the cap.
