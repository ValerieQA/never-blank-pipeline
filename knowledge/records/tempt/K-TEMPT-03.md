# Invented scene

## Statement
The tempting inadmissible interpretation supplies a scene the evidence does not
have: a morning, a dashboard, an inbox, a person noticing something. It is
written in the present tense and reads as reporting, so a reader takes it as
something that happened. Nothing supports it, and I-03 refuses it outright.

## Probe
Is there a reading that supplies a scene — a moment, a place, a gesture, a
reaction — that no source observation records?

## Real examples
- `reports/content_packages/0c3ab0c08576373a_generated.json`, generated
  2026-09-17, Facebook post: "Imagine waking up, checking your business
  analytics, and seeing a sudden, unexplained drop in new customer inquiries and
  website visits." The evidence is a report about publisher traffic; the
  morning, the dashboard and the drop are the engine's.
- `docs/editorial/CANONICAL_EDITORIAL_MAP_v1.md` §13 walkthrough A records
