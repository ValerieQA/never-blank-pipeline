# Transfer to the reader

## Statement
The tempting inadmissible interpretation moves the case's outcome onto the
configured reader: what happened to the party in the evidence is written as
what happens to the person reading. The evidence is scoped to that party and
says nothing about the reader's own conditions, so the transfer has no support
at any strength.

## Probe
Is there a reading that transfers the case's outcome, its costs or its feelings
onto the configured reader, without evidence scoped to that reader?

## Real examples
- `reports/content_packages/a71d52f27d921529_generated.json`, generated
  2026-09-18, from a single documented case of one seller reaching $150,000 in
  two weeks: "You’ve hustled for every sale, crafted every post, and dreamed of
  the day your household product would catch fire online." Nothing in the
  evidence is about the reader.
- `docs/editorial/CANONICAL_EDITORIAL_MAP_v1.md` §13 walkthrough A records the
  same move as "you, the reader, bake at night", and the boundary refused it.

## Applies when
always

## Influences
- S-04 · `E-09.inadmissible` — the probe's finding is recorded as tested.

## Conflicts
None known. A consequence *for* the reader is a legitimate interpretation kind
(E-08 `consequence_for_reader`) when the evidence supports it at the reader's
scope; this family is about the reading that skips that support.
