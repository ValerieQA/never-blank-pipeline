# Entity fields a record may shape (Step 1)

`## Influences` says which stages a record may shape and **which fields of
which entity**. A field is written in backticks and qualified by its entity:
`` `E-13.reveal` ``. A name that is not here is refused (§8 rule 5) — a record
that claims to shape a field nobody has is a record nothing can apply.

Only the decided fields are here. Identifiers, refs, versions and outcome
records are structure the engine owns; no knowledge record shapes them.
