# Plan formats (E-14 `format`)

What the destination plan is shaped as. A condition reads it as
`format is <name>`.

## Terms

- `article` — the long canonical form.
- `post` — a single social post.
- `carousel` — ordered slides.
- `thread` — an ordered sequence of posts.
- `channel_post` — a channel message.
- `other` — anything the five above do not describe. A plan that uses it says
  what it is in `first_line_mechanics`.
