# Audience Profile attributes

The part of the Audience Profile a condition may read, as
`audience <attribute> is <value>`. The Audience Profile is client setup
material; what a record may condition on is this closed list.

## Terms

- `english_level`: global / native — whether the reader reads English as a
  second language or as a first.
- `cultural_context`: international / local — whether the reader shares the
  subject's own market context.
