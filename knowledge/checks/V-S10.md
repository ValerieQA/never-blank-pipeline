# What the destination's own ranking knowledge says about this text

## Rule
A text that goes against the ranking knowledge recorded for its destination — a
link in the body of a Facebook post, a hashtag block unrelated to the image, a
first line that carries no specific — is worth noticing. It is never a reason to
block, edit or replan anything.

## Criterion
Code applies the `K-DST-*` records loaded for this destination that are
descriptive rather than hard policy, and records each one the text goes
against, with the record's id, tier and effective status. A record demoted to
`weak-candidate` still produces a hint and nothing more. Hard platform policy
is not this check's business: a tier-1 rule is V-P04 at the plan and S-14 at
publication.

## Enforces
Nothing. Map §9.2 V-S10. It is where `K-DST-FB-01`, `K-DST-IG-01`,
`K-DST-LI-01`, `K-DST-TH-01`, `K-DST-TG-01` and `K-DST-WIX-01` reach a finished
text, and its findings are what S-15 later compares with the measured reach.
