# The Echo reframes the opening rather than recapping it

## Rule
The ending returns to the image or fact the opening used and leaves it meaning
something it did not mean at the start. An ending that restates the opening, or
restates the article, is a recap and fails.

## Criterion
The execution call judges the approved `ending_intention` on its own first: read
as written and executed faithfully, would it return to the opening with a new
meaning, or would it repeat what the reader already has? Then it judges the
text against the same question. The criterion is the change in meaning, not the
repetition of words: an Echo reuses the opening's material on purpose.

## Enforces
Map §9.2 V-T08, and the client's `ending_mode` where one is declared — for
Never Blank, the article ends on the Kicker and nothing follows it.

## Branch criterion
The execution call judges the approved `ending_intention` on its own, against
this check's criterion, **before** it judges the text. The intention fails →
`strategy`: the plan asked for an ending that could only recap, and S-08 owns
it. The intention passes and the text fails → `execution`: the Writer had a
valid reframe to execute and wrote a recap, and it edits against the same
approved plan. When uncertain, the plan branch (Step 2 §5.3).
