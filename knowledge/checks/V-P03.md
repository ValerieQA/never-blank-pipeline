# The unit's destinations carry one anchor and do not contradict each other

## Rule
Every plan of the unit carries the unit's anchor interpretation, and no two of
them assert things a reader meeting both would have to choose between.

## Criterion
One call per unit per round, at barrier B1, receives the anchor and every
destination's plan. It answers two explicit questions. First, per plan: is the
anchor the thing this plan asks the reader to believe, or has the plan drifted
to a different reading? Second, across plans: does any pair assert facts or
consequences that cannot both be true? A failure names the deviating
destination, because only that destination goes back.

## Enforces
I-07 — one unit, one anchor — through map §9.1 V-P03. Destinations stay
siblings (I-06): the check compares them against the anchor, never against each
other's texts, and it never makes one the source of another.
