# An ending that adds nothing to the thesis

## Rule
An ending that leaves the reader exactly where the thesis already put them is
worth noticing. It is never a reason to block, edit or replan anything.

## Criterion
The execution call receives the thesis and the ending and answers one question:
after the ending, does the reader hold anything the thesis did not already give
them — a consequence, a limit, a changed meaning? "No" is the finding. Where
the ending is an Echo, this check asks about progression and V-T08 asks about
recapping the opening; a text can fail one and pass the other.

## Enforces
Nothing. Map §9.2 V-S08 (formerly H02). It is the soft companion of V-T08: the
hard check owns recapping, this one owns going nowhere.
