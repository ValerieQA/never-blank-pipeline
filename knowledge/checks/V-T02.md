# The text expresses no inadmissible interpretation

## Rule
The text asserts nothing the boundary recorded as inadmissible, and nothing the
boundary never admitted at all.

## Criterion
The truth call receives the text, the Evidence Core and the boundary's
admissible list, and answers one explicit question per reading the text puts
into a reader's head: is this reading supported by the evidence as scoped, and
is it in the admissible list at the version this text was planned against? The
recorded inadmissible list is given as a detection aid only. **The check is not
bounded by that list** (patch R1): a reading nobody predicted still fails, and
it becomes a KnowledgeQueueItem proposing a new probe family (U-1 feedback).

## Enforces
I-05, through map §9.2 V-T02, which inherits invariant status from it: the
detection may be probabilistic, the rule may not.
