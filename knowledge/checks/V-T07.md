# The text executed the approved plan

## Rule
The opening, the reader path and the concession of the approved plan are in the
text, doing what the plan said they would do.

## Criterion
The execution call receives the approved plan and the text, and answers three
explicit questions. Does the text open as the plan's `opening` declares, holding
back what the plan holds back? Does it execute the moves of `reader_path`, in
order, each carried by the segment the plan assigned it? Is the `concession`
present, where the plan placed it, conceding the limitation it names? A move
executed somewhere else is a finding; a move missing is a finding.

## Enforces
Map §9.2 V-T07, and the plan-is-the-gate half of I-08. The plan owns the
structure, so a structural failure goes back to S-08 rather than to the Writer.
