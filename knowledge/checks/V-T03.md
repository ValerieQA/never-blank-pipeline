# The chain from thesis to observation is unbroken

## Rule
`editorial_thesis → interpretation → evidence_claim → source_observation`
resolves at every link, and the text stands on that chain rather than beside it.

## Criterion
Code walks the references in the approved plan and fails any link that does not
resolve: a thesis with no interpretation, an interpretation with no evidence
claim, a claim with no observation. The truth call answers the other half: does
the text actually rest on the chain the plan declares, or does it carry the
thesis on something else — an aside, an analogy, a reader assumption?

## Enforces
I-04, through map §9.2 V-T03, which inherits invariant status from it. Where
V-T01 asks whether a fact is in the core, this asks whether what the text
asserts is held up by it.
