# The strategy's fields hold together

## Rule
The fields of one strategy are consistent with each other and with what the run
already established: the reveal fits the format's length, an opening built on a
figure has that figure in the core, a reader path that makes a case the subject
of a move needs `documented_case` confirmed in the core, and the second
interpretation the plan leans on is in the boundary's admissible list.

## Criterion
Code answers the parts that are lookups: is the opening's figure in the Evidence
Core, is the second interpretation admissible, does the length target allow the
declared reveal. The model answers one explicit question about the rest: taking
the fields as written, is there a text that satisfies all of them at once? A
"no" names the two fields that cannot both hold. Path labels are never read
(I-10): the check compares fields, and the label is written after the decision.

## Enforces
Map §9.1 V-P01. It is the plan-side half of I-08 — decisions before prose — and
it keeps S-12 from being handed a plan no text can execute.
