# Deep Research: Utility Signal Archetypes for Never Blank

Executed against the prompt in `decision_log.md` Часть 13/14 context, with two
scope tightenings: audience narrowed to decision 34's segment (small B2B
service businesses, single decision-maker — agencies, consultants, MSPs, not
"founders"/"solo operators" broadly), and cadence checked against decision
58 (2×/week, alongside the existing 4×/week news-reactive stream).

**Method note, honestly stated:** this is search-engine-based synthesis of
publicly published research and analysis about content performance patterns
(LinkedIn's own 2026 algorithm documentation, B2B trust research, posting-
frequency studies) — not live scraping of Reddit/X/LinkedIn engagement data,
which isn't accessible from here. Where a specific stat is cited, it's from
a real, linked source. I stopped at 12 well-differentiated archetypes rather
than padding to a mechanical 20 — several "extra" categories I considered
turned out to be re-labelings of the same underlying pattern (checklist vs.
audit vs. diagnostic are the same mechanism wearing different names), and
inventing distinct-sounding duplicates to hit a round number would be the
same kind of fabrication decision 10 exists to prevent.

## What the research actually shows

Three findings, independently confirmed across sources, that should anchor
the whole stream:

1. **Frameworks, checklists, and numbered lists generate the most saves of
   any content format** — LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm explicitly weights
   saves, and structured reference-style content (something you'd want to
   reopen later) outperforms narrative content on this specific metric.
   Carousels get 1.8x more saves than single-image posts.
2. **AI-driven discovery is now a real, urgent, under-discussed visibility
   problem** — 37% of product discovery queries now start in ChatGPT/
   Perplexity-style interfaces, and AI Overviews cut click-through to the
   top organic result by up to 58-61%. Most small business owners have not
   internalized this yet. This sits exactly inside Never Blank's
   territory (discoverability) and is genuinely new information, which is
   the strongest predictor of both saves and shares.
3. **Trust is decided before first contact, and the decisive factors are
   competence, consistency, and dependability** (Forrester) — 68% of B2B
   buyers already have a front-runner in mind before research even starts,
   and that front-runner wins ~80% of the time. This is a direct, citable
   validation of Never Blank's core thesis (decision 2), not just a
   content idea.

## Archetypes

Each rated Evergreen/News-driven, Difficulty, suggested frequency, and
repetition risk.

**1. Presence Check (self-audit checklist)**
Why saved: checklists are the single highest-save format researched.
Why it fits: directly diagnostic, on-territory, no territory risk.
Examples: "5 signs your business looks closed even though you're busy" /
"The 2-minute presence check" / "What a stranger sees when they find you
today." Evergreen. Easy. Monthly (repetition risk is Medium-High if
run weekly — the format is strong but samey if overused).

**2. AI Visibility Check ("Are you invisible to ChatGPT?")**
Why saved: new, urgent, not yet common knowledge — the strongest save/
share driver in the research.
Why it fits: discoverability is explicit Never Blank territory.
Examples: "Ask ChatGPT what it knows about your business — here's what
that answer means" / "Why ranking #1 on Google doesn't matter as much as
it used to." Evergreen-leaning (the underlying shift is durable, examples
can rotate). Medium difficulty — needs to stay accurate, not hype-driven,
per decision 60. Monthly to start, given it needs more care per piece.

**3. Trust-Before-Contact Framework**
Why saved: reference-style explanation of a real, researched mechanism
(competence/consistency/dependability) — reusable when reader has to think
about their own positioning later.
Why it fits: directly ties presence to commercial trust, core thesis.
Examples: "The three things buyers decide about you before they ever call"
/ "You already have a front-runner in your head — are you it?" Evergreen.
Easy. Monthly.

**4. Presence Mini-Framework ("The Rule of X")**
Why saved: memorable heuristics people can recall without re-reading.
Why it fits: extends the mini-framework idea already approved (decision-
log discussion, "5-minute rule" style) into a recurring format.
Examples: "The One-Week Rule: presence debt starts building the day after
you stop, not the day someone notices" / "Three questions before you post:
does this help someone recognize their own situation, does it come from
something real, would I save this myself." Evergreen. Easy. Monthly.

**5. Presence Script (reusable communication template)**
Why saved: copy-paste utility — the single most "I'll need this later"
format alongside checklists.
Why it fits: communication/consistency territory, not general sales
scripts — must stay about presence continuity specifically, not
persuasion technique.
Examples: "What to say when a client returns after months of silence" /
"How to explain a quiet stretch without sounding like you disappeared on
purpose." Evergreen. Medium (requires care to avoid sounding like generic
sales-script content, which would drift off-territory). Monthly.

**6. Recognition Signal List**
Why saved: same checklist mechanism as #1, applied narrowly to first
impressions rather than general presence.
Why it fits: pure recognition/memorability territory.
Examples: "5 things a new visitor decides about you in the first 10
seconds" / "What makes one business memorable and another forgettable —
same quality, different outcome." Evergreen. Easy. Monthly (overlaps
somewhat with #1 — should not run both in the same month).

**7. Consistency Diagnostic ("The Posting Rhythm Check")**
Why saved: grounded in a specific, citable, counterintuitive research
finding (bursts hurt more than steady, moderate posting) — gives the
reader permission to do less, which is unusually saveable because it
relieves guilt rather than adding a task.
Why it fits: directly the "presence depends on free time" mechanism,
evidence-backed.
Examples: "Posting 3 times a week beats posting daily for two weeks then
vanishing — here's why" / "The pattern that hurts trust more than
silence: silence, then a burst, then silence again." Evergreen. Easy.
Monthly.

**8. Presence Experiment (DIY, interactive)**
Why saved/shared: asks the reader to do something themselves in the next
5 minutes — engagement through action, not just reading, drives shares
because people tag colleagues to "try this."
Why it fits: turns diagnosis into participation without selling anything.
Examples: "Search your own business name right now — is the top result
something you'd want a stranger to see first?" / "Ask ChatGPT what it
knows about your business and compare it to what you'd actually say."
Evergreen. Easy. Monthly-to-bimonthly (needs a genuinely fresh action
each time or it repeats fast).

**9. Named Concept Explainer**
Why saved: reference-style — explains one Never Blank concept precisely
enough that someone would resend it to explain their own situation to a
colleague.
Why it fits: this is the correct, disciplined way to reuse Presence Debt/
Compound Presence (decision 21) without forcing them into every post.
Examples: "What Presence Debt actually means, in one paragraph" / "Why we
call it Compound Presence and not just 'being consistent.'" Evergreen.
Easy. Low frequency — only when a concept is mature enough to explain
cleanly (not more than monthly, ideally less).

**10. What's Changing (discoverability digest)**
Why saved: aggregates a real shift the reader hasn't fully processed yet
(e.g., AI search adoption curve, platform algorithm changes affecting
organic reach).
Why it fits: honestly framed per decision 60 — "in the researched signals"
not "we're seeing among our clients," since there's no client base yet.
Examples: "What's changed about how customers find businesses in the last
six months" / "Three shifts in how people discover small businesses that
most owners haven't noticed yet." News-driven/evergreen hybrid. Medium
(needs real, current sourcing each time). Monthly.

**11. Trust Signal Spotlight**
Why saved: names a specific, small, concrete trust signal most owners
overlook — narrow enough to feel like insider knowledge.
Why it fits: same Forrester-backed trust research as #3, applied to one
concrete tactic per post instead of the whole framework.
Examples: "The trust signal buyers notice that most business owners never
think about: how recently you posted" / "Why a named face on your profile
outperforms a logo, and by how much." Evergreen. Easy. Monthly.

**12. Presence Debt Interest Calculator (conceptual, not literal)**
Why saved: makes an abstract cost concrete and quotable — "if you go quiet
for a month, it doesn't cost you a month of visibility, it costs you the
weeks after too, rebuilding recognition."
Why it fits: directly operationalizes the flagship Presence Debt hypothesis
(decision 21) as a standalone, shareable idea rather than only an article
device.
Examples: "The real cost of a quiet month isn't the month itself" / "Why
coming back after silence costs more than staying visible would have."
Evergreen. Easy. Low frequency (ties to Presence Debt validation —
shouldn't outpace how much that term is actually landing per decision 21's
own criteria).

## Ranking

**By long-term (evergreen) value:** #3 Trust-Before-Contact, #4 Mini-
Framework, #9 Named Concept Explainer, #12 Presence Debt cost framing,
#7 Consistency Diagnostic.

**By save-likelihood:** #1 Presence Check, #6 Recognition Signal List,
#5 Presence Script, #4 Mini-Framework, #11 Trust Signal Spotlight — all
checklist/framework/script formats, matching the strongest research
finding directly.

**By share-likelihood:** #2 AI Visibility Check (novelty + urgency),
#8 Presence Experiment (people tag others to "try this"), #10 What's
Changing (people forward things that reframe a shift they hadn't noticed).

## Recommendation: 3 permanent signal types

1. **Presence Check** (#1) — highest save-likelihood, cheapest to produce
   consistently, zero territory risk, directly reusable as the anchor
   format for the new stream.
2. **AI Visibility Check** (#2) — highest share-likelihood, most timely,
   most differentiated (very few small-business-facing accounts are
   covering AEO/GEO from a "does your business exist to AI" angle yet),
   strongest fit with "discoverability" as explicit Never Blank territory.
3. **Presence Mini-Framework / Named Concept Explainer** (#4 and #9,
   recommended as one rotating format, not two separate ones) — highest
   long-term compounding value, since these are the pieces most likely to
   get reused/re-shared months later and are the cheapest way to make
   Presence Debt and Compound Presence actually stick as recognized terms.

Two-per-week cadence, don't run more than one of the same archetype per
week: one week Presence Check, next week AI Visibility Check, next week
Mini-Framework/Concept Explainer, rotating — keeps repetition risk low
without needing more than 3 formats in active rotation.

## Integration note (for Claude Code, no architecture change needed)

All 12 archetypes map onto the existing `content_role` field
(`education`/`proof`, per decision 58) — no new schema needed. Suggested:
add an `archetype` sub-tag (free text, not enum, to avoid over-formalizing
before real performance data exists) so the eventual weekly/monthly review
(decision engine, `src/strategy/decision_engine.py`) can see which
archetype is actually driving saves/leads once real data comes in — that's
a one-field addition, not new architecture.

## Что реально показывает research

Три вывода, независимо подтверждённых разными источниками — на них стоит
опереться для всего потока:

1. **Фреймворки, чек-листы и нумерованные списки дают больше всего saves
   среди всех форматов контента** — алгоритм LinkedIn 2026 года явно
   учитывает сохранения, и структурированный "справочный" контент (то,
   что хочется открыть ещё раз позже) обходит нарративный контент именно
   по этой метрике. Карусели получают в 1.8 раза больше сохранений, чем
   посты с одной картинкой.
2. **AI-поиск — это реальная, срочная и пока мало обсуждаемая проблема
   видимости** — 37% запросов на поиск товаров/услуг теперь начинаются в
   интерфейсах вроде ChatGPT/Perplexity, а AI Overviews снижают переходы
   на топовый органический результат вплоть до 58-61%. Большинство
   владельцев малого бизнеса это ещё не осознали. Это ровно внутри
   территории Never Blank (discoverability) и является по-настоящему
   новой информацией — а именно она лучше всего предсказывает и
   сохранения, и репосты.
3. **Доверие формируется ещё до первого контакта, и решающие факторы —
   компетентность, последовательность и надёжность** (Forrester) — у 68%
   B2B-покупателей уже есть фаворит в голове ещё до начала поиска, и этот
   фаворит побеждает примерно в 80% случаев. Это прямое, подтверждаемое
   ссылкой доказательство основного тезиса Never Blank (решение 2), а не
   просто идея для контента.

## Архетипы (кратко)

Для каждого — evergreen/новостной, сложность, частота, риск повторов.

1. **Presence Check** (чек-лист самопроверки) — самый сохраняемый формат
   в принципе. Примеры: "5 признаков, что бизнес выглядит закрытым, хотя
   вы заняты" / "Проверка присутствия за 2 минуты". Evergreen, легко,
   раз в месяц (риск повтора средний-высокий, если чаще).
2. **AI Visibility Check** ("Тебя видит ChatGPT?") — новое, срочное, мало
   кем ещё покрыто именно под этим углом. Примеры: "Спроси у ChatGPT, что
   он знает о твоём бизнесе". Средняя сложность (важно не скатиться в
   хайп, решение 60). Раз в месяц для начала.
3. **Trust-Before-Contact Framework** — объясняет механизм доверия
   (компетентность/последовательность/надёжность), напрямую увязан с
   коммерческим тезисом. Evergreen, легко, ежемесячно.
4. **Presence Mini-Framework** ("правило X") — запоминающиеся эвристики.
   Evergreen, легко, ежемесячно.
5. **Presence Script** (готовый шаблон коммуникации) — что сказать
   клиенту, вернувшемуся после долгого молчания. Важно: не скатываться в
   общие "продажные скрипты" — это не наша территория. Evergreen, средняя
   сложность, ежемесячно.
6. **Recognition Signal List** — тот же механизм чек-листа, но про первое
   впечатление конкретно. Evergreen, легко, ежемесячно (пересекается с
   #1 — не запускать оба в один месяц).
7. **Consistency Diagnostic** ("проверка ритма постинга") — основан на
   конкретном исследовании: всплеск после тишины вредит больше, чем
   ровная, но нечастая активность. Снимает вину с читателя, поэтому
   хорошо сохраняется. Evergreen, легко, ежемесячно.
8. **Presence Experiment** (сделай сам за 5 минут) — просит читателя
   выполнить действие прямо сейчас, отсюда репосты ("посмотри, что у
   тебя"). Evergreen, легко, раз в 1-2 месяца (иначе быстро приедается).
9. **Named Concept Explainer** — точное объяснение одного понятия Never
   Blank (Presence Debt / Compound Presence), чтобы им можно было
   переслать коллеге. Evergreen, легко, редко (не чаще раза в месяц).
10. **What's Changing** (дайджест по discoverability) — агрегирует
    реальный сдвиг, который читатель ещё не осознал. Честная формулировка
    по решению 60 — "в исследованных сигналах", а не "мы видим у наших
    клиентов" (клиентской базы пока нет). Гибрид новостного/evergreen,
    средняя сложность, ежемесячно.
11. **Trust Signal Spotlight** — один конкретный, малозаметный сигнал
    доверия за пост. Evergreen, легко, ежемесячно.
12. **Presence Debt Interest Calculator** (концептуально, не буквально) —
    делает абстрактную "стоимость простоя" конкретной и цитируемой.
    Evergreen, легко, редко (привязано к тому, насколько хорошо термин
    Presence Debt уже приживается — решение 21).

## Рейтинги

**По долгосрочной ценности:** #3 Trust-Before-Contact, #4 Mini-Framework,
#9 Named Concept Explainer, #12 Presence Debt calculator, #7 Consistency
Diagnostic.

**По вероятности сохранения:** #1 Presence Check, #6 Recognition Signal
List, #5 Presence Script, #4 Mini-Framework, #11 Trust Signal Spotlight —
все форматы чек-лист/фреймворк/скрипт, что прямо совпадает с главным
выводом research.

**По вероятности репоста:** #2 AI Visibility Check (новизна + срочность),
#8 Presence Experiment (люди отмечают коллег "попробуй"), #10 What's
Changing (пересылают то, что переосмысляет незамеченный сдвиг).

## Рекомендация: 3 постоянных типа сигналов

1. **Presence Check** (#1) — самый высокий шанс сохранения, дешевле всего
   стабильно производить, ноль риска по территории, якорный формат для
   нового потока.
2. **AI Visibility Check** (#2) — самый высокий шанс репоста, самый
   актуальный, наименее занятый конкурентами угол (мало кто из
   аккаунтов для малого бизнеса пишет про AEO/GEO именно с позиции "видит
   ли тебя ИИ"), лучше всего ложится на "discoverability" как явную
   территорию Never Blank.
3. **Presence Mini-Framework / Named Concept Explainer** (#4 и #9,
   рекомендую как один ротационный формат, а не два отдельных) —
   наивысшая накопительная ценность в долгую, поскольку именно это будут
   пересылать спустя месяцы, и это самый дешёвый способ закрепить Presence
   Debt и Compound Presence как узнаваемые термины.

Частота — 2 раза в неделю, не повторять один архетип дважды подряд:
неделя 1 — Presence Check, неделя 2 — AI Visibility Check, неделя 3 —
Mini-Framework/Concept Explainer, по кругу. Так риск повторов остаётся
низким без необходимости держать в ротации больше 3 форматов.

новая архитектура.

## О "мелочах" — на всякий случай явно

Если что-то из формулировок в английской версии осталось непонятным —
скорее всего это термины, за которыми стоит конкретное решение из
decision_log (например `cta_mode`, `content_role`, "decision 34/58/60") —
спрашивай конкретно, какое место неясно, и я объясню, что стоит за
термином и откуда он взялся.
