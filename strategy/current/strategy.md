---

## What we are doing

Making small B2B service business owners recognize that their visibility depends on when they have free time — not on a system. And showing them the commercial cost of that pattern.

Never Blank sells itself through the quality of this analysis.

---

## Who we are talking to

Small agencies (digital/web/dev/creative), independent consultants, and MSPs.  
Single decision-maker. Busy delivering client work. Often going dark exactly when they need new clients.

---

## The problem we are naming

**Owner-dependent presence.**

When the owner is at capacity, visibility collapses. That is exactly when the next project should be coming in. The business becomes invisible to the market at the moment it most needs to be visible.

---

## Our hypothesis

If we can make owners recognize their own presence pattern — and feel the commercial cost of it — they will want a system that maintains presence without depending on their free time.

That system is Never Blank.

---

## What the articles must do

- Make the reader think: *"That is happening to us."*
- Then: *"This is why it happens — it is structural, not motivational."*
- Then: *"The cost is real — we are losing pipeline at the moments we most need it."*
- Then: *"Consistent presence is not about posting more. It is about not disappearing."*

Articles sell through quality of thinking, not direct pitch.

---

## Flagship concept: Presence Debt (Campaign 1)

**Definition:** The accumulated cost of the periods when a business disappears from customers' view and has to rebuild recognition, familiarity, and trust again.

Being tested this phase. Not forced into every article. Tracking whether readers adopt the term.

---

## CTA intent this month

`reflection` — primary. Invite recognition, not action.  
Distribution varies by article: some `none`, some `reflection`, occasional `diagnostic`.  
No hard sell. The reader is at the beginning of recognizing the pattern.

---

## Success criteria

- **Primary:** At least 2 qualified leads before the review date
- **Early signals:** Posts generating saves + self-recognition comments; Presence Debt concept repeated back by readers
- **Minimum evaluation period:** 4 weeks of consistent publishing

---

## When this strategy continues vs. changes

**Continue:** Any lead generated, or consistent positive engagement trend showing self-recognition.

**Adjust execution:** Direction confirmed, but specific hooks/topics/platforms not working. Change those without changing the core strategy.

**Replace:** No leads after 6+ weeks, no recognition signals, no positive trend despite variations.
