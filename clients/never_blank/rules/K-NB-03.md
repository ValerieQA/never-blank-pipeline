# Another publication's coined term is never presented as Never Blank's own

## Statement
A term somebody else minted is attributed to whoever minted it, every time it
is used. Never Blank mints its own or reuses its own; it does not adopt a
borrowed phrase as though the thinking behind it were its own.

## Applies when
always

## Influences
- S-12 · `E-15.text` — the term is attributed where it is used.
- S-13 · `E-15.text` — V-T01 reconciles the attribution with the core.

## Conflicts
None known. `clients/never_blank/lenses/portable_noun.md` decides when Never
Blank mints a term at all; this record decides what happens to a term that is
already somebody else's.
