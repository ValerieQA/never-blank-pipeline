# A consequence is stated with a number or as something falsifiable

## Statement
Where the evidence has a number for the consequence, the text states the
consequence with that number. Where it has none, the text states a concrete
consequence a reader could check and find wrong. A benefit that cannot be
wrong — "more efficient", "better positioned" — is not a consequence.

## Applies when
always

## Influences
- S-08 · `E-13.editorial_thesis` — what the text asserts, and how checkably.
- S-12 · `E-15.text` — the consequence as written.
- S-13 · `E-15.text` — V-T01 reconciles the number with the core.

## Conflicts
None known. Where the number exists but reaching only level 1 or 2 on the
strength ladder, the ceiling decides how strongly it may be put; this record
decides that it is put at all, not how far it may be stretched.
