# A social text adds nothing the article does not establish

## Statement
A text for a social or platform destination may reframe, shorten and rephrase
what Never Blank has established. It may not add a fact, a figure, a cause or
an author's experience that is not established. Reaching a shorter format is a
matter of what is left out, never of what is brought in.

## Applies when
not (destination is wix)

## Influences
- S-10 · `E-14.citations` — a derivative cites only what the core holds.
- S-12 · `E-15.text` — the Writer adds nothing to reach the length.
- S-13 · `E-15.text` — V-T01 and V-T04 reconcile the text with the core.

## Conflicts
As written for the current engine this rule says a post carries only what the
**accepted article** contains. I-06 (tier A) says destinations are siblings and
no text is the source of another: in the target each destination is planned
from the Evidence Core and the Interpretation Boundary, not from the Wix text.
The tier-A invariant wins on where the material comes from. What this rule
keeps at tier 2 is its actual force — a destination text asserts nothing that
the run has not established — and that is what V-T01 and V-T02 enforce.
