# The article ends on the Kicker, and nothing follows it

## Statement
The ending returns to the image or fact the opening used and leaves it meaning
something new, and the text stops on that line. Nothing comes after it: no call
to action, no question to the reader, no sign-off.

## Applies when
always

## Influences
- S-08 · `E-13.ending_intention` — what the ending is for.
- S-10 · `E-14.segments` — the last segment is the Kicker and carries nothing else.
- S-13 · `E-15.text` — V-T08 judges the ending against this.

## Conflicts
The editorial source material in `editorial/reference/` still describes a call
to action after the Kicker. Owner decisions #266 and #268 supersede it, and
this record is the superseding rule.
