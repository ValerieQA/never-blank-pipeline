# Monday — CLIENT: NEVER_BLANK

What Monday is for, and which signals it may start from. Owner decisions D1 and
D8 in #240. Edit here: nothing else holds these rules.

- `## Purpose` reaches the writers of the Wix article and the LinkedIn post.
- Every bullet under `## Selection` is a rule a candidate signal is judged
  against. The first signal that satisfies all of them — together with any lens
  routed to selection, such as `lenses/evidence.md` — is used, and no later
  candidate is judged.

Keep the two headings `## Purpose` and `## Selection` exactly as written. See
`../README.md` for how this file is read.

## Purpose

Monday is not a news-recap engine. A factual event is raw material for a
practical Never Blank angle for small business: what does this change for a
small business, what practical mechanism does it reveal, and what should an
owner notice before copying, buying, building, automating, or reacting? The
value is in how Never Blank interprets and writes the signal, not in which
category the signal belongs to.

## Selection

### Useful

- A signal may come from any subject area or source class — news, regulation,
  product release, research, market change, operational example, technology,
  finance, policy, culture, customer behavior, or something else — as long as
  it can support a genuinely relevant and interesting practical angle for small
  business.
- Judge the angle the signal supports, not its category. There is no list of
  permitted signal types: never reject a useful signal because of its source
  class alone.
- AI and small business is an excellent, on-brand subject when it appears, but
  it is not a required class and never a reason to pass over another strong
  signal.
- A major story everyone is already discussing and a fresh development few have
  noticed are equally acceptable.
- A generic advice or listicle source with no new mechanism, observation,
  tension, or practical value is not useful merely because it is about small
  business.
