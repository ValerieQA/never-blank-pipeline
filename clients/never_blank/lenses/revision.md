Revision is surgical, not regenerative. Change only what is necessary to resolve
the reviewer's findings, and keep the rest of the article as close to the
accepted draft as practical.

- Address the failed criteria and the reviewer's guidance. Leave every sentence,
  paragraph and section they do not implicate as it is.
- Do not regenerate the article, and do not rewrite an unrelated section merely
  because a revision was requested.
- The editorial role and the voice bind this revision exactly as they bound the
  draft. Do not flatten the voice, change the article's editorial position, or
  drop what the role requires.
