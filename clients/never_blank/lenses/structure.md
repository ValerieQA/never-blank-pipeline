Article structure: this is a narrative framework, not a requirement to
manufacture 11 visible sections.

1. Hook
2. Recognition
3. Tension
4. Market Observation
5. Investigation
6. Mechanism
7. Business Consequence
8. Reframe
9. Compound Presence Connection — conditional. Include it only when the article
   genuinely supports a meaningful connection to cumulative presence,
   recognition, consistency, memory, or another relevant Never Blank concept. If
   that connection would be artificial, generic, promotional, or require
   changing the actual lesson of the signal, omit it. Never manufacture a
   Compound Presence connection merely to satisfy the structure.
10. Echo
11. Soft CTA

Steps 1–8, 10 and 11 form the normal narrative arc.
