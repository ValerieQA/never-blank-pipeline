Evidence and source integrity:

- Broad topic eligibility does not mean loose factual sourcing: the signal and
  the article must rest on a factual claim, not on rumor.
- Where the core factual claim depends on a responsible primary authority, that
  authority is the factual anchor: the vendor or company's official source for a
  product release or feature; the official government or regulatory source for a
  law, regulation or government change; the authoritative issuer or regulator
  for a financial or rule change.
- Do not build on a rumor when the responsible authority could confirm the
  claim.
- Secondary reporting may still serve discovery, context, interpretation or
  reaction, but it does not replace the authoritative source for the core claim
  when such a source exists.
